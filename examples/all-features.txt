All-features circuit
====================

This is one sheet that uses every part, so you can try the whole bench
on a circuit that is already known.

Open it from the Anaconda Prompt, after the usual update:

    conda activate base
    cd C:\Users\danni\OneDrive\Documents\Python Scripts\PyCircuit
    git pull
    python main.py

Then File > Open and choose:

    examples\all-features.json

The sheet is large. View > Zoom to Fit shows all of it.

Supplies
--------

BATT is +12 V. Its plus node is named Vcc.
NEG is the -12 V rail. Its minus node is named Vee. Plus of NEG is ground.
The ground symbol is named Vss. Every Vss point is ground.
V10 is a separate 10 V source for the dividers only.
IN is the one AC source: 1 V peak, 1 kHz, no offset.

V(s)
----

This sheet has a diode, an LED, transistors, MOSFETs, a comparator,
logic gates and a 555, so there is no single s-domain formula. The DC
numbers from Simulate > DC Operating Point still show. A sheet with
only the resistors, capacitors and sources would show V(s).

DC operating point
------------------

Simulate > DC Operating Point. These are the checks:

- The three 10k / 10k dividers on V10 read 5 V in the middle.
  Change the bottom resistor of one to 30k and that middle becomes
  7.5 V. A source, a pot position, or a switch does the same: the
  DC numbers, and any plot already showing, run again.
- The wire that hops the vertical ground wire reads 6 V. Without that
  bridge the same point would be ground.
- The potentiometer is at position 0.5, so the wiper is about 6 V.
  The 10 megohm resistor on the wiper pulls it down by a hair.
- The transformer is 2 turns. The primary is 12 V, so the secondary
  is 24 V.
- The 1 mA source into 1 kilohm is 1 V.
- The closed switch reads 12 V on the load. The open switch reads
  about 0 V.
- The regulator is set to 5 V, and its 1 kilohm load is at 5 V.
- The 555 control pin is about 8 V (two thirds of 12 V, pulled a
  little by the 1 megohm resistor).
- NOT, buffer, AND, OR, NOR and XOR outputs are 12 V. NAND is 0 V.
  The comparator output is 12 V.
- The LED current is about 9.5 mA, so a scope run lights the LED.

Probes
------

P1  IN, the AC source. Direct.
P2  the low-pass output (1 kilohm and 159 nF). Direct.
P3  the generic op-amp follower output. Direct.
P4  the voltage-follower block. Direct.
P5  the non-inverting amplifier, gain 2. Direct.
P6  the inverting amplifier, gain 2. Direct.
P7  the plain divider, 5 V. Direct.
P8  the second divider. 1 Mohm to ground.
P9  the third divider. 10x scope probe.
P10 differential, the top of the plain divider minus the middle.
    That is 10 V minus 5 V.
P11 current in the top resistor of the plain divider, 0.5 mA.
P12 current in the LED's resistor.

The 1 Mohm probe and the 10x probe do not change the DC operating
point. They load the circuit when the oscilloscope or Bode runs.
On its own divider, 1 Mohm reads 4.975 V and the 10x probe reads
4.9975 V.

Oscilloscope
------------

View > Oscilloscope, then Start. Uncheck P7 through P12 if the flat
lines crowd the sine. P1 is 1 V at 1 kHz. P2 is the low-pass, about
0.7 V at that frequency and -45 degrees. P3 and P4 copy P1. P5 is
twice P1. P6 is twice P1, upside down. The LED lights.

Bode
----

View > Bode, then Start. From 100 Hz to 10 kHz is enough. Leave P1
through P6 checked.

- P1 is 0 dB.
- P2 is about -3 dB and -45 degrees at 1 kHz.
- P3 and P4 are about 0 dB.
- P5 is about +6 dB.
- P6 is about +6 dB, phase 180 degrees.

P7 through P12 are DC points. Uncheck them so the plot stays on the
sine.

FFT
---

View > FFT is a tab on the oscilloscope. Run the oscilloscope first,
with P1 through P6 checked. P1 is a 1 V line at 1 kHz. P2 is about
0.7 V on that same line.

Cursors
-------

The oscilloscope, Bode, and FFT each have two dashed lines, A and B.
Drag them. The box reads both, and the difference is B minus A.

On the oscilloscope, put A on a zero of P1 and B 250 us later. That
is a quarter cycle of the 1 kHz sine, so the difference is about 1 V.
On the FFT, put A at 0 Hz and B on the 1 kHz line. The difference is
about 1 V. On Bode, put A at 100 Hz and B at 1 kHz on P2. The
difference is about -3 dB and -39 degrees.
